## What this test does

Chrome against the website built from the current code, with its own pages published for three namings: the starter names (about / contact), the console names 'About Us' / 'Contact Us' (about-us / contact-us), and pages whose address is something else but whose menu label says About Us / Contact Us. In every case: the header's menu (computer and phone) has Pricing and Apps but nothing called About or Contact; the footer's Company column has both; the 'Talk to us' button still goes to the contact page. The phone menu: pressing Menu opens it; pressing Pricing, FAQ, Apps or the 'Talk to us' button goes to the page AND closes the menu (it used to stay open on top of the new page); a link to the page that is already open closes it too; pressing outside, Escape, pressing Menu again and the browser's back button all leave it closed. With JavaScript off the menu still opens (a native <details>).

## Steps

1. About Us / Contact Us are in the footer, not in the header menu
2. The phone menu closes after a link is pressed
3. Without JavaScript the menu still opens

## What to expect (these must happen)

- About Us and Contact Us are not in the header menu, on a computer or a phone, whatever they are called in the console.
- They are in the footer's Company column, and 'Talk to us' still reaches the contact page.
- The phone menu closes after any link, a press outside or Escape.

## What must NOT happen

- The menu must NOT stay open after a link is pressed.
- The menu must NOT stop opening when JavaScript is off.
- No errors in the browser.
