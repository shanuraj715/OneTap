## What this test does

Runs suites/staff-app-menu-api.mjs on a private API with an in-memory database (no browser). A store with one of every kind of dish: plain, switched off, counted stock at zero, counted but running low, counted with plenty, outside its serving hours, and a combo with a part that is off. GET /api/menu/unavailable lists exactly the ones that can't be ordered, each with the words to show; a dish that is only running low is reported separately with how many are left; the answer is a small fraction of the whole menu. Switching a dish on or adding stock takes it off the list (and the combo that needed it); switching one off puts it on at once. A dish on the list is refused when priced for an order and one that is not on it can be priced (the same rule). The menu carries a version that does NOT change when stock counts change but DOES change with a new price or a new dish, and the unavailable answer carries the same version.

## What to expect (these must happen)

- Exactly the dishes that can't be ordered now are listed, with their labels; everything else is absent (absent = can be ordered).
- The list follows the restaurant: switching on / stock added removes a dish, switching off adds it.
- The menu version changes for a price or a new dish, not for stock counts.

## What must NOT happen

- A dish running low or with plenty left must NOT be listed as unavailable.
- A dish the order check refuses must NOT be missing from the list.
- The whole menu must NOT be needed to know what is unavailable.
