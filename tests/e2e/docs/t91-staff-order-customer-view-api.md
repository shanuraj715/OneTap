## What this test does

Runs suites/staff-order-customer-view-api.mjs on a private API with an in-memory database (no browser). A waiter takes an order at a table for 9000000001; the customer signs in on the storefront with that number (no sign-up questions — the record exists) and finds the order in their orders list with its items, its status (Accepted — staff orders go straight to the kitchen) and 'not paid'; the tracking page follows the kitchen (Accepted → Ready, with the steps) and the list says paid once the waiter confirms payment; a second round at the same table appears too. Another customer sees none of it and cannot open it (404), someone not signed in gets 401, the same number written with +91 and spaces reaches the same orders, and a customer's Remove hides the order from THEIR list only.

## What to expect (these must happen)

- The customer sees the staff-taken order, its items, status and payment in My orders after signing in with the same number.
- The tracking page follows the status live.

## What must NOT happen

- Another customer must NOT see or open it.
- Removing it from the customer's list must NOT remove it from the restaurant.
