# T102 — Storefront: a customer who signed in stays signed in for 180 days, through reloads, new tabs and a server that can't answer for a moment (restart, deploy, rate limit, dropped connection) — without being sent back to the login form

**Area:** storefront · **Last result:** ⚪ not run yet

**Run it alone:** `node run.mjs --only t102` (add `--visible` to watch it)

## What this test does

Chrome against the bench's Acme store. The customer signs in once with a code; the cookie is HttpOnly and lasts 180 days. After a reload and in a new tab the checkout opens signed in. Then the 'who am I?' call is made to fail twice — with a 502, with a 429 and with a dropped connection, one after the other — and each time the login form never appears (the page waits and retries, a little longer each time) and the checkout comes up signed in as soon as the server answers. A clear answer of 'nobody is signed in' still shows the login form.

_The steps are recorded the first time the test runs._

## What to expect (these must happen)

- A customer signed in once is still signed in after a reload and in a new tab; the sign-in cookie lasts 180 days.
- A failed check of the sign-in (502, 429, no connection) is retried and never shows the login form.

## What must NOT happen

- A hiccup of the server must NOT send a signed-in customer back to the login form (and cost another code).
- A real 'nobody is signed in' answer must NOT be hidden: the login form still shows.
