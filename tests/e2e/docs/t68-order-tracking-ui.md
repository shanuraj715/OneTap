## What this test does

Runs suites/order-tracking-ui.mjs in Chrome on the bench storefront. A signed-in customer's order page /main/order/<id>: Placed with 'waiting for the restaurant to accept' and a step bar; when staff accept it the page changes to Accepted by itself (no reload), 'Check now' looks straight away, Ready says it can be collected, 'What happened' lists each step with its time, items and total are shown, and once Completed it stops refreshing. A cancelled order says Cancelled and what that means for the money, with no progress bar. The Account page has 'Follow this order →' for an order still going; the real 'Order placed' screen (ordering on the storefront) has a 'Follow your order' button to that order's own page. Another customer opening the link sees 'We can't find that order' and nothing of it; someone not signed in is asked to sign in; on a phone the page doesn't scroll sideways.

## What must NOT happen

- Another customer's link must NOT show the order.
- A finished or cancelled order must NOT keep refreshing.
- No errors in the browser.
