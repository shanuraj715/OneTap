## What this test does

In Chrome: the platform creates a plan without Marketing and a plan with it, and a store on the first. The owner finds no Marketing in the sidebar and, opening its address directly, a 'Marketing isn't in your plan' page with no forms. The platform then unlocks Marketing for that one store (Features tab) and the owner gets the real Marketing screen; it is set back to the plan, the store moves to the plan that has it (still works), and finally Marketing is locked for the store even though its plan has it (the lock wins) and the owner loses it again.

## Steps

1. Platform console → Plans: one plan without Marketing, one with it
2. Two stores on the plan WITHOUT Marketing
3. The owner of the Basic-style store: no Marketing
4. The platform unlocks Marketing for THIS store only
5. Back to 'from plan', then the store moves to the plan that includes Marketing
6. Locking Marketing for one store beats its plan
7. Other Pro stores are untouched

## What to expect (these must happen)

- The plan form has a 'Marketing' switch; a plan without it shows no Marketing to its stores.
- On a plan without Marketing the owner's sidebar has no Marketing entry, and opening /marketing/new says 'Marketing isn't in your plan' — no channel cards, no 'Review and send'.
- Unlocking Marketing for that one store (platform → store → Features) gives the owner the Marketing item and the working screen, without changing the plan.
- Moving the store to a plan that includes Marketing gives it Marketing.
- Locking Marketing for the store overrides its plan: the owner loses it again; setting it back to 'From plan' restores it.

## What must NOT happen

- A store whose plan lacks Marketing must NOT see the Marketing menu item or the sending screens.
- An unlock for one store must NOT give Marketing to other stores on the same plan.
- A lock for one store must NOT remove Marketing from other stores on the Pro plan.
- The browser must NOT log JavaScript errors.
